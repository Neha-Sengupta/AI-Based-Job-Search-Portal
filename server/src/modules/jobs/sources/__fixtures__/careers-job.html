Legal Tech Associate, Lexlegis.ai

Mumbai | Full Time | On Site

Experience: 2 to 3 years

Company Description

Lexlegis.ai is an AI-powered legal intelligence platform built for the legal and tax domains. Combining advanced artificial intelligence with deep legal expertise, the platform helps lawyers, tax professionals, in-house legal teams, enterprises, and government bodies research, draft, and make informed decisions more efficiently. At Lexlegis.ai, legal professionals and technologists work together to build innovative solutions that transform how legal work is performed.

Role Description

We’re looking for a tech-savvy legal professional who understands legal workflows, enjoys solving problems, and is comfortable working at the intersection of law and technology. You’ll work closely with different teams, take ownership of projects, help improve our AI-powered legal technology products, and ensure ideas and requirements translate into effective solutions.

This is a hands-on, cross-functional role for someone who takes initiative, adapts quickly, and enjoys figuring things out. You’ll have the opportunity to contribute to product development, improve legal data operations, enhance customer experience, and support the successful delivery of product initiatives.

Day-to-day responsibilities include:

  • Working with Technology and other internal teams to translate requirements into product improvements, coordinate development, test features, and support their implementation
  • Coordinating legal data operations to ensure the timely collection, maintenance, accuracy, and quality of legal information
  • Supporting customers by coordinating the resolution of product-related issues and developing help guides, training materials, and feature walkthroughs
  • Enabling the Sales team through product training, feature updates, client demonstrations, and coordination of client documentation and issues
  • Identifying gaps, improving processes, and coordinating across teams to ensure projects progress smoothly and product capabilities meet customer needs

Qualifications

  • 2 to 3 years of relevant work experience in a law firm, an in-house legal team, legal technology, or a similar role
  • An undergraduate degree in Law (LL.B.) is the minimum qualification
  • An understanding of legal workflows and an interest in legal technology and AI-powered solutions
  • Strong technical aptitude and comfort working with digital platforms, software tools, and technology-driven workflows
  • Strong communication, analytical, organisational, and problem-solving skills
  • The ability to manage multiple priorities, coordinate with different teams, and take ownership of work independently
  • A proactive, adaptable approach and the ability to work effectively in a fast-paced environment

Good to Have

  • Experience working with legal technology products, AI-powered platforms, or software-driven legal workflows
  • Exposure to product management, customer support, legal data operations, or cross-functional project coordination
  • Familiarity with product documentation, feature testing, and user training

What We Offer

  • Work at the intersection of law and AI. Contribute to building technology solutions for the legal industry.
  • Broad exposure. Work across product development, legal data, customer experience, and business operations.
  • Ownership and impact. Take responsibility for projects and contribute to improvements that directly affect product quality and customer experience.
  • Room to grow. Develop your understanding of product management and legal technology while working closely with legal and technical teams.

If you enjoy solving problems, learning new technologies, and turning ideas into practical solutions, we’d like to hear from you.
